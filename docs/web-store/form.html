Description
Prophylaxis is a free chess coach for chess.com players. When you finish a game, it reviews it beside the board, on your own computer.

AFTER EVERY GAME
• The move the game turned on, with your winning chances before and after.
• Accuracy and move grades for both players.
• Retry your key moments: find the better move yourself before seeing the answer.
• What your mistake allowed, and how many players at your rating find the better move ("about 1 in 50").

ACROSS ALL YOUR GAMES (on the Prophylaxis website)
• Your #1 habit, such as moving too fast or leaving pieces undefended, with how often it shows up.
• Practice built from positions you got wrong, brought back on a spaced-repetition schedule.
• Your opening tree, and a comparison with any other player.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no server. The Stockfish chess engine runs on your computer.
• Your games and reviews stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
• The extension contains no analytics.

FAIR PLAY
• It never helps during a game. A game is only reviewed once chess.com has published it in your public archive of finished games.

Privacy practices

Single purpose description
Reviews the chess.com games you finish, in Chrome's side panel next to the board, once each game is over.
storage justification
Saves your chess.com username, two settings (whether the panel opens by itself, and the Prophylaxis website address), and your last 15 game reviews, on your computer.
sidePanel justification
Shows the review of the game you just finished in Chrome's side panel, next to the board.
offscreen justification
Runs the Stockfish chess engine in Web Workers to analyse a finished game. A service worker can't start Web Workers, so the analysis runs in an offscreen document.
Host permission justification (one box covers all sites)
api.chess.com: Downloads the game you just finished from chess.com's public game archive, which only lists finished games. www.chess.com: Notices when you are on the page of a game that has ended (from the page address and chess.com's game-over card), and shows a small card with the review's result. It never reads the board or moves from the page. chess.com changes pages without reloading, so the script has to be present on all its pages to notice a game page. atefmo-1.github.io/prophylaxis: Lets the Prophylaxis website, when you open it, pick up the reviews this extension made and send back your habits. This stays inside your browser.
